## 6. Cycle-accurate simulation and latency/energy figures

**Status:** not present in the repository.

The manuscript reports latency (8.2 ms), energy (2.8 mJ), and memory (29.0 KiB) for ecg_hybrid_8bit. The repository does not contain a cycle-accurate simulator script, a trace of cycle counts, or an energy model. The only related constants in llm_nas/model_weights.h are M_CODE_BYTES = 12,288 (12.0 KiB), M_ACTIVATION_PEAK_BYTES = 11,014 (10.756 KiB), M_RUNTIME_BYTES = 2,560 (2.5 KiB). These are declared constants, not simulated or measured values.

**Conclusion:** the latency and energy figures in the manuscript are estimated, not the output of a cycle-accurate simulation. This must be stated explicitly in any future revision. It is an open item for Stage 6.2.
